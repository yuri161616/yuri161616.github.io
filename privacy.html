Privacy policy

Prepared: 19 November 2026 · Game: Crystal Combo · Android package: io.github.yuri161616.crystalcombo

Release status

Crystal Combo is being prepared for Google Play. The current Google Play test builds, versions 107 and 108, contain two separate optional rewarded-ad integrations: Google AdMob for eligible adults and AwesomeAds by SuperAwesome for the younger category. Both advertising routes are disabled in these test builds: players cannot load an advertisement or receive a new advertising reward. The conditional descriptions below explain the integrated components; they do not mean advertising is currently available. Development builds use provider test advertisements, which do not earn advertising revenue. Live child advertising requires publisher approval, a rewarded placement, updated published disclosures and device testing before activation.

Who publishes the game

CrystalCombo publishes Crystal Combo. For questions or requests about privacy, contact supportcrystalcombo@gmail.com. This address is also the game's support contact.

Information kept on your device

The game stores progress, stars, scores, unfinished runs, booster balances and preferences locally. An optional play journal records gameplay events locally when enabled. The game does not create a player account, provide cloud progress synchronisation or upload this progress to a server operated by CrystalCombo.

You can choose to export a progress file or play journal using Android's file picker. The destination you select, such as a cloud storage provider, has its own privacy practices. Exported progress does not include every setting or unfinished run.

Children and age information

The game is intended for a mixed audience, including children. In builds with optional advertising enabled, a neutral age screen offers two equally presented choices: "Under 18" and "18 or older", without a default selection. Players can skip and continue playing. This is a product routing rule, not proof of age or a statement of legal adulthood in every country. The younger category uses a separate child-directed advertising service; it never uses the adult Google rewarded-ad or consent path. Unknown, skipped, unrecognised or incompletely saved age choices cannot request either kind of advertisement.

The game stores only the chosen category and whether the question was answered, locally. It does not ask for an exact age or date of birth. A local interruption marker keeps advertising blocked if saving a changed answer fails. The category and marker are excluded from exported progress and the play journal. Legacy restricted answers remain on the more protective route; the game does not infer an exact age from them. Changing the category cancels loaded and pending advertisements. Players can review the age setting when advertising is enabled.

Optional advertising and rewards

When the relevant advertising service is enabled, players may explicitly load and then watch an advertisement for the reward named before viewing: two hammer uses, or one meteorite, three extra moves, undo, recolour, next-piece choice or a temporary falling-speed freeze. These boosters unlock after level 10. Watching an ad is not required to play. The game does not automatically show an ad after loading, on launch, after losing, or while a level is actively running. A level is paused before its booster inventory opens. Closing early returns to the game; a reward is saved only after the advertising provider reports that it was earned. Clicking an advertiser link or installing anything is not required for a reward. There is no reward for merely opening an ad or waiting for a local timer.

Adult route: Google AdMob

The adult route uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for required privacy choices before requesting an ad. The game requests non-personalised advertising with maximum content rating G. Non-personalised does not mean no data processing: Google's services may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad/app interactions, diagnostics and identifiers allowed by the configuration for delivery, measurement and security. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. Required ongoing privacy choices remain accessible in the game's settings and booster inventory. Changing a privacy choice does not itself request a new ad.

Younger route: AwesomeAds by SuperAwesome

When enabled, this route uses contextual child-directed rewarded video from SuperAwesome. The SDK starts only after an explicit eligible request to load an ad. No exact age, birth date, contact details, player identifier or game progress is supplied by the game as custom advertising metadata. The provider still processes technical request data such as IP address, user agent, app identifier, device class, language and ad/performance events, and caches advertising files. The SDK also creates a random key in local app storage and derives an app-scoped value from that key, the app identifier and the current month for its first-party audience measurement/capping mechanism. The derived identifier is sent with ad requests as a provider-generated app-scoped DAU value. This is distinct from the Android advertising ID, but it means this integration must not be described as processing no identifiers at all. Confirmation of the provider's server-side use and retention of this value is part of the production activation review. Contextual advertising is not a promise that no data leaves the device. See the Awesome Ads Marketplace Privacy Policy for its handling of data and privacy request routes.

The SDK's own early-close control and Android Back are enabled, and no game-built overlay is placed over the advertisement. Closing before an earned event grants no reward and does not remove existing boosters or game progress. The provider may have documented completion or compensation rules; the game relies on its reward event instead of inventing a viewing timer. A parental gate protects opening an advertiser's external destination. That gate is not parental consent to collect personal data, and the game does not ask the child to approve an adult advertising-consent form.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. Email is handled by our email provider, Google. Do not include passwords, identity documents or payment-card information in support messages.

This website

The official website at yuri161616.github.io is hosted using GitHub Pages. These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, to serve and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Reading the policy bundled inside the game does not require visiting the website; opening an external link connects to that destination.

Storage, deletion and your choices

  • Local game information remains on your device until you remove it. You can turn off and clear the optional play journal in the game. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes its local data.
  • Exported files are separate copies. Delete them from each storage destination yourself if you no longer want to retain them. Clearing app data does not delete exported files.
  • Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request, follow up on unresolved issues and meet applicable obligations. You can ask us to delete it at the support address; we will explain any information that must be retained and why.
  • Google and SuperAwesome control retention of information processed by their respective services under the linked policies. Clearing local game data does not automatically delete information already held by a provider. Use the provider's privacy controls or contact us for help identifying the appropriate request route.
  • The game has no player account to delete. You can contact support with access, correction or deletion requests concerning information you have sent us.

Security and international processing

We aim to collect only information needed for the stated purpose and limit access to support correspondence. Google states that advertising SDK data is encrypted in transit. Services and hosting providers may process information outside your country under their applicable policies. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's data handling changes and revise the date above. Material changes will also be described through the game or its release information where appropriate.
