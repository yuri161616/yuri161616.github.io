Privacy policy

Prepared: 18 November 2026 · Game: Crystal Combo · Android package: com.neonblocks.game

Release status

Crystal Combo is being prepared for Google Play. Version 97 includes optional Google advertising and consent components. Only eligible adults can choose to load an ad from a booster inventory; no ad loads automatically when the game starts. Ad availability also depends on Google account and app approval, connectivity and ad inventory. Development builds use Google's test advertisements. Earlier builds may have advertising disabled.

Who publishes the game

CrystalCombo publishes Crystal Combo. For questions or requests about privacy, contact supportcrystalcombo@gmail.com. This address is also the game's support contact.

Information kept on your device

The game stores progress, stars, scores, unfinished runs, booster balances and preferences locally. An optional play journal records gameplay events locally when enabled. The game does not create a player account, provide cloud progress synchronisation or upload this progress to a server operated by CrystalCombo.

You can choose to export a progress file or play journal using Android's file picker. The destination you select, such as a cloud storage provider, has its own privacy practices. Exported progress does not include every setting or unfinished run.

Children and age information

The game is intended for a mixed audience, including children. In versions with optional advertising enabled, a neutral age screen offers two choices: "Under 18" and "18 or older", with neither selected in advance. Players can skip the question and keep playing. Optional advertising is for eligible adults only. Version 97 uses 18 as the game's minimum advertising age; this product rule does not establish legal adulthood or replace applicable local age and privacy requirements. The app does not start advertising or consent SDKs, request ads or request advertising consent for players below this threshold or players whose age is unknown. Skipping the question leaves advertising unavailable.

The age screen does not ask for an exact age or date of birth. It saves only an eligibility category and whether the question was answered, locally on the device. An existing answer that met the earlier minimum age of 21 remains eligible; an existing restricted answer stays restricted until the player explicitly answers again in settings. The app also keeps a local interruption marker while saving an age choice. The marker contains no age or identifier and keeps advertising unavailable if that change is interrupted. Age choices and this marker are not included in exported progress or the play journal. No exact age or date of birth is sent to the developer or an advertising provider. Players can review the age setting when the advertising feature is enabled.

Optional advertising

When advertising is enabled, eligible adults can choose to load and watch an advertisement for the reward identified before the ad: two hammer uses, or one use of a meteorite, three extra moves, undo, recolour, next-piece choice or a temporary falling-speed freeze. These boosters unlock after level 10. A booster inventory can be opened from the map or during a voluntarily paused level. Watching ads is not required to play; the game never opens an advertisement automatically or while a level is actively running. A completed reward is saved in the local inventory and does not need to be used immediately. Loading ads requires internet access. The game uses Google AdMob for ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for required privacy choices. The app begins this flow after an eligible player chooses to load an ad, rather than loading an ad automatically on launch. It requests non-personalised ads with a maximum ad content rating of G and waits for the required consent process before requesting an advertisement.

Non-personalised does not mean that no data is processed. Google's advertising services may process an IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad and app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers permitted by the configuration. These data support delivery, measurement and security.

Google's handling of advertising data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. The consent service may process privacy choices and technical information needed to display and remember the relevant choice. Where Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry, it is available in the game's settings and booster inventories. Changing a privacy choice does not itself load a new ad.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. Email is handled by our email provider, Google. Do not include passwords, identity documents or payment-card information in support messages.

This website

The official website at yuri161616.github.io is hosted using GitHub Pages. These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, to serve and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Reading the policy bundled inside the game does not require visiting the website; opening an external link connects to that destination.

Storage, deletion and your choices

  • Local game information remains on your device until you remove it. You can turn off and clear the optional play journal in the game. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes its local data.
  • Exported files are separate copies. Delete them from each storage destination yourself if you no longer want to retain them. Clearing app data does not delete exported files.
  • Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request, follow up on unresolved issues and meet applicable obligations. You can ask us to delete it at the support address; we will explain any information that must be retained and why.
  • Google controls retention of data processed by its advertising and consent services under its own policies. Clearing game data does not automatically delete information already held by Google. Use Google's privacy controls or contact us for help identifying the appropriate request route.
  • The game has no player account to delete. You can contact support with access, correction or deletion requests concerning information you have sent us.

Security and international processing

We aim to collect only information needed for the stated purpose and limit access to support correspondence. Google states that advertising SDK data is encrypted in transit. Services and hosting providers may process information outside your country under their applicable policies. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game's data handling changes and revise the date above. Material changes will also be described through the game or its release information where appropriate.
